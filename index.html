How Sculpture works

Point your camera at one person and slowly walk around them for 10–30 seconds. Sculpture reconstructs the subject on your device as a photorealistic 3D capture you can orbit, zoom, and share as a Gaussian splat file (.ply or .spz). No account is required and nothing is uploaded — all processing happens on your device.

Tips for great scans

  • Film one person, framed head to toe, with room to walk around them.
  • Move slowly and keep a steady circle around the subject.
  • Choose even lighting and a simple background.
  • Hold the phone in portrait, roughly at chest height.
  • Keep your device awake until the reconstruction finishes.

Troubleshooting

  • “Camera tracking failed.” Film a slower orbit with better lighting and try again.
  • “Couldn’t separate the person from the background.” Keep the subject clearly in frame against a simple background.
  • “Not enough memory to finish.” Try again, or use a shorter video.
  • “Not enough storage on this device.” Free up space and try again.
  • A scan didn’t turn out well? Every video stays in your Library — tap Rebuild scan to reconstruct it again any time.

Sculpture requires iOS 16 or later.
