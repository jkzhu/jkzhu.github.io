Privacy Policy

Sculpture · Effective November 18, 2026

The short version: Sculpture does not collect, transmit, or sell your personal data. The app contains no network communication at all — your videos and 3D scans never leave your device unless you personally share or back them up.

Who we are

This policy describes the app Sculpture for iPhone and iPad. For any privacy questions, contact us at zhujianke@outlook.com.

Data we do not collect

Sculpture has no servers and no accounts. The app does not communicate over the internet: there is no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party SDK that sends data anywhere. In App Store Connect terms, our privacy label is “No Data Collected,” and this policy explains why that is literally true.

What happens on your device

  • Camera. Sculpture asks for camera access only to record a short orbit video of a person — the subject of your 3D scan. Recording happens locally.
  • Microphone. Optional audio may be captured while you record a scan video. It stays on your device.
  • Photo library (if used). If a video is selected from your photo library, only the single video you pick is read, and it is processed on your device.
  • Reconstruction. Turning your video into a 3D capture is completely on-device computation using your iPhone’s or iPad’s own GPU. No frames, images, point clouds, or models are uploaded.

Where your data lives, and how you delete it

Your scan videos and reconstructed 3D captures are stored only inside Sculpture’s private folder on your device. You control them:

  • Delete any scan or video in the app’s Library at any time; deletion removes it from the app permanently.
  • If you use iOS backups (iCloud or your computer), anything in the app may be included in a backup you initiated; manage or disable that in your device settings.
  • If you use the app’s share features (for example, exporting a .ply or .spz file), only the specific file you choose is handed to the app or service you pick — that transfer is between you and that service, not us.

Deleting the app removes everything it stored on the device.

Children

Sculpture is a general-audience tool. Because it collects no personal data from anyone, it also collects none from children. Anyone photographed or filmed with the app should consent — please use the app responsibly.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the effective date at the top of this page will be updated. Material changes will be announced in the app.
